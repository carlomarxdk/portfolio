% ================= PROFILE =================
\needspace{3\baselineskip}
\cvsection{Profile}
Postdoctoral researcher (PhD, Applied Mathematics and Computer Science) specializing in statistical and machine-learning modeling of registry-scale health \& behavior data, and in the robustness, and stability assessment of AI systems. Investigated transformer-based systems for nation-scale health/socioeconomic trajectory prediction (\href{https://life2vec.dk}{life2vec}, trained on national registry and electronic health records), veracity probing of LLMs and multi-agent LLM simulation (CoevolveSim), with end-to-end Python experience from data extraction and cleaning to modeling and interpretability analysis. Works across multidisciplinary teams and mentors graduate researchers in interpretable ML and AI auditing.

\textbf{Areas of interest:} population-scale health \& behavior modeling, explainable AI \& AI auditing, bias \& robustness assessment, transformer architectures, agent-based simulation, evaluation of predictive models.
